Frequently asked questions

Everything you're probably wondering before you request a quote.

How much does it cost to hire a portable toilet in Milton Keynes?

Standard single portaloo hire typically runs from around £45–£75 per week depending on hire length, servicing frequency and delivery distance. Weekend event hire and luxury toilet trailers are priced separately. Tell us what you need and we'll get you a fixed quote, usually within 2 hours.

How quickly can a unit be delivered?

Most standard bookings are fulfilled within 48 hours, and we can often arrange next-day or same-day delivery for urgent site or event needs across Milton Keynes and nearby towns.

Are the units cleaned and serviced during hire?

Yes. Long-term hires are serviced on a weekly schedule as standard, including waste removal, restocking and a full clean. Event hire units are cleaned and checked before every delivery.

What's the difference between a standard portaloo and a luxury toilet trailer?

A standard portaloo is a single cabin unit, ideal for building sites and casual events. A luxury toilet trailer has multiple cubicles, flushing toilets, running water, mirrors and interior lighting — better suited to weddings and formal parties.

Do I need a permit for site toilets in Milton Keynes?

Usually not for placement within a private site or driveway. If a unit needs to sit on a public footpath or road, a temporary licence from Milton Keynes City Council may be required — we can talk you through this when you enquire.

What areas do you cover?

We cover Milton Keynes and the surrounding area, including Bletchley, Wolverton, Stony Stratford, Newport Pagnell, Olney, Woburn Sands, Buckingham, Leighton Buzzard, Bedford and Aylesbury. See our areas page for more detail.

How do I request a quote?

Fill in the short form on our quote page with your dates, location and a few details about the job. We'll come back with a fixed price, usually within 2 hours during working hours.
