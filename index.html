How to play

A mystery word is hiding. Your job: close in on it by meaning.

1

Guess any word

No word list. Type anything: nouns, verbs, ideas. We measure how close it is in meaning to the answer.

2

Read the temperature

0° is arctic, 100° is boiling. If today's word were ocean, "house" would be freezing, "beach" hot and "sea" scorching.

3

Beat the cooling

Hesitate and the puzzle loses heat: 1° every 2 seconds and 2° every guess. The bar shows both your true meaning match and what cooling has taken, so you always know where you really stand. Stripes on the thermometer show heat lost to cooling: 1° every 2 seconds and 2° every guess.

4

Reach your degree

Solve it and you score 100° minus cooling minus hint costs (5° for the first hint, 10° for the second, 15° for the third), never below 10°. Solving always beats giving up.

Good to know

One puzzle a day. Everyone gets the same word, and it changes at midnight UK time (Europe/London, summer time included).

Everything stays on your device. Scoring runs in your browser using a compact language model, so there's no account and no server. Your streak and results live in this browser's storage.
